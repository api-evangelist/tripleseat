For hospitality groups, the holiday season is one of the most important revenue opportunities on the calendar. Corporate celebrations, client dinners, family gatherings, and end-of-year events can put private dining rooms and event spaces in high demand. According to Tripleseat’s own data, Holiday booking inquiries jump 5–6x between October and November.
